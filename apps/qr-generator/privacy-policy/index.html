Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

Introduction

The QR Generator application is built and maintained as a Free app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone who decides to use this service.

Information Collection and Use

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: This application does not require any user account creation, registration, or sign-in.
  • Local Device Operations: The application functions entirely locally on your mobile device. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information or user data.
  • Camera Permission: The application may request access to your device's camera. This permission is utilized solely for scanning QR codes within the app interface. The video feed and scanned image data are processed locally on your phone and are never transmitted to any external servers.

Log Data and Third-Party Services

  • No Tracking: This application does not use background tracking services, analytics tools, or third-party SDKs that collect data on your device, behavior, or location.
  • No Advertisements: No advertising network software is integrated into this application.

Children’s Privacy

This application is designed to be safe and appropriate for users of all ages, including children. Because the application operates entirely locally on the device, it does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, location data, or sensitive data from children or users of any age. We are fully committed to complying with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
