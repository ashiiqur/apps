Privacy Policy

Introduction

The QR Generator application is built and maintained as a free app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is designed to work on your device. We do not run any server that receives your data.

Summary

  • There is no account, registration or sign-in required to use the app.
  • Your scan and generation history stays on your device unless you choose to export it or turn on Google Drive sync.
  • The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your data. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • History: Codes you generate or scan (the text or link inside the code, an optional label and label color, the code type and the time) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone so you can see them again. Codes you delete move to a Recycle Bin and are removed permanently after 30 days.
  • Settings: Your preferences, such as sound, scan options, the last tab you used, and an optional backup name for your device, are stored locally.
  • Wi-Fi QR codes: If you create a Wi-Fi QR code, the network name and password you enter are used only to build the code on your device and are kept in your history like any other code. Delete the entry if you do not want it stored.
  • Images you save: PNG, JPG and SVG files are saved only when you tap Save, to your Pictures or Downloads folder.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used only to scan QR codes and barcodes. The camera image is processed on your phone by an on-device scanner and is never sent to us or to any server. The camera permission is optional; you can also scan from a photo you choose from your gallery.
  • Photos you pick: When you choose "Upload Image to Scan" or add a logo to a code, the app reads only the image you select.
  • Notifications: Used for reminders about Google Drive sync and about a scheduled "Delete all data". You can decline this permission.
  • Internet and start-up: Internet access is used only for the optional Google Drive sync and for opening links you choose. The app may restart its background tasks (Recycle Bin cleanup and sync) after the phone restarts.

Optional Google Drive Sync

Google Drive sync is off by default. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account and allow the app to use its own hidden "app data" folder in your Google Drive. The app can access only that folder, not your other Drive files.

  • What is synced: Your history (including the Recycle Bin), your settings and your backup name, saved as a single file in your own Google Drive.
  • Who can see it: The file lives in your Google account. The developer cannot see it or access it. Google handles it under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Account information: The app reads the email address of the Google account you select only to show which account is in use, and keeps that on your device.
  • When it runs: After you change your history, about every 6 hours in the background, and when you tap "Sync now", only while sync is on.
  • Turning it off or deleting: You can turn sync off at any time. The "Delete all data" option removes the history on your phone and the synced file in your Drive after a short waiting period during which you can cancel.

Backup and Restore

  • Export and restore: You can export your history to a file in a location you choose, and restore it later. The file is created only when you ask, and it is yours to store or delete.
  • Android Auto Backup: If Android backup is enabled on your phone, your history and settings may be included in your Google backup so they can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, not by the developer. Device-specific data and Google Drive sync state are not included.

Third-Party Services

  • Google services: The app uses Google's on-device barcode scanning library (ML Kit), Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API (only if you enable sync). Scanning runs on your device. Google may process limited technical data as described in its own policies.
  • No tracking: The app does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not collect your location.
  • Links: If you open a link from a scanned code, it opens in your browser or another app. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and the developer is not responsible for them. Only open codes you trust.

Data Retention and Your Choices

  • Delete single items, multiple items or all history inside the app.
  • Empty the Recycle Bin, or let items expire after 30 days.
  • Turn off Google Drive sync, or revoke the app's access from your Google Account permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone. Exported files and your Google Drive copy are removed only if you delete them (or use "Delete all data" before uninstalling).

Security

Your history is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google Drive uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your phone and Google account protected.

Children's Privacy

This application is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The developer does not receive any data from the app. If you use the optional Google Drive sync, your Google account's own terms and age requirements apply. We are committed to complying with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when the app adds features. You are advised to review this page periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
