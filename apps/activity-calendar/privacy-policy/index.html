Activity Calendar
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone. If you choose to turn on Google Drive sync, a copy is saved in a private folder in your own Google Drive.

I don’t run any servers, I can’t see your data, and the app has no ads and no tracking.

Who I am

Activity Calendar is an Android app made by Ashiqur Rahman (“I”, “me”). If you have a question about this policy, write to me at email@ashiqur.in.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your phone, in the app’s private storage. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Your work sessions (the start and end time of each session).
  • Your reminders (title, details, date and time).
  • Your name, if you type one in the Profile tab.
  • Sync settings: whether sync is on, the time of the last sync, and which Google account you used for it.

I do not receive any of this. It never leaves your phone unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off until you tap “Turn on Google Drive sync” and approve it on Google’s own screen.

  • The app asks for one permission, drive.appdata. It lets the app read and write a single file in a hidden folder in your Google Drive. Only this app can open that folder.
  • The app cannot see your other Drive files, photos or documents.
  • The file contains the same data listed above: sessions, reminders and your name.
  • Data goes directly between your phone and Google. It does not pass through any server of mine.
  • Sync runs shortly after you make a change, when you open the app, and about every 6 hours in the background. This lets several of your phones stay in step.

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: only to sync with your Google Drive when sync is on.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders at the time you set.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts or the time or time zone changes.

What I do not do

  • I do not collect, store or see your personal information.
  • I do not show ads and I do not use analytics or crash-tracking tools.
  • I do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • The app has no account or sign-up of its own.

Third-party services

When sync is on, the app uses Google Play services and Google Drive. Google’s handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

If you use Android’s own device backup, Android may also include the app’s data in that backup. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by me.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Stop syncing: open the Profile tab and tap “Turn off”. Your data on the phone and in Drive stays as it is.
  • Remove the Drive copy: in the Google Drive website, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Activity Calendar and choose “Delete hidden app data”.
  • Take back access: open myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Activity Calendar.
  • Remove data from your phone: delete individual sessions and reminders in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the device.

Children

The app is not made for children, and because I collect no personal information, I do not knowingly collect any from children.
