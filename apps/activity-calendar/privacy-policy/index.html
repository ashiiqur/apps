Privacy Policy

Introduction

The Activity Calendar application (shown as “Activity” on your phone) is built and maintained as a Free app. It is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. It combines a work timer, a calendar, tasks, notes and home screen widgets. This page is used to inform users regarding the policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information for anyone who decides to use this service.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your Content: The application stores what you create inside it: work sessions and the activities they belong to, tasks and their reminders, notes (including drawings), the groups they are in, and the items waiting in the Trash.
  • Your Profile: If you choose to, you can enter a name and pick a profile photo. A photo is chosen with the Android photo picker, which gives the app only the single image you select and never your whole gallery.
  • Settings: Small preferences, such as the tab you last used, sort orders and which activities are starred or ticked, are kept on the device so the app opens where you left off.
  • Stays on Your Phone: All of this is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive it, cannot see it, and operates no server that collects it. No account with the developer is needed to use the app.

Optional Google Drive Sync

Google Drive sync is off until you turn it on from the Profile tab. It lets you keep the same data on more than one phone and keep a backup.

  • Your Own Drive: When sync is on, the application asks you to sign in with a Google account and to allow access to the Drive app data folder only (the drive.appdata permission). This is a hidden folder reserved for this app inside your own Google Drive. The application cannot see or change your other Drive files, and other apps cannot read this folder.
  • What Is Uploaded: A single backup file containing your work sessions, activities, tasks, notes and drawings, groups, Trash items, your name and your profile photo (if you set them).
  • Your Email Address: The application reads the email address of the signed-in Google account only to show you which account is syncing. It is kept on your device.
  • No Developer Access: Data travels directly between your phone and Google. It does not pass through any server operated by the developer, and the developer cannot read your backup.
  • Your Control: You can turn sync off at any time. “Change email” moves the sync to another account and, once the new account has synced, deletes the old account’s backup. “Delete all data” removes your data from the phone and from the Google Drive backup (see below).
  • Google’s Policies: Signing in and storing the backup are handled by Google and are covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the sync and backup feature, and is never used for advertising or sold.

Permissions

  • Internet: Used only for Google Drive sync, and to open the links you tap in the app. The application makes no other network requests.
  • Notifications: Used to show task reminders at the time you set. They are scheduled and shown on your device.
  • Run at Startup: Used to set your reminders again after the phone restarts, and to update the calendar widget when the phone’s time or time zone changes.
  • Not Requested: The application does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, or storage permissions.

Home Screen Widgets

The calendar widget, the Add activity (+) widget and the Starred activities widget show information from the app on your home screen and let you start or stop the timer, pick activities, or add an activity. They read data stored on your device and do not send anything anywhere.

Log Data and Third-Party Services

  • No Tracking: This application does not use analytics tools, crash-reporting services, advertising identifiers, or background tracking of your behavior or location.
  • No Advertisements: No advertising network software is integrated into this application.
  • Google Play Services: The only third-party component involved in handling data is Google’s sign-in and authorization library, used solely for the optional Drive sync described above.

Deleting Your Data

  • Trash: Deleted tasks, notes, groups and work sessions wait in the Trash for 30 days so you can restore them, then are removed for good. You can also remove an item or empty the Trash yourself at any time.
  • Delete All Data: In the Profile tab, “Delete all data” removes your work sessions, tasks, notes, name and photo from the phone and from the Google Drive backup of the signed-in account. It starts after a short countdown, during which you can cancel it.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the application deletes the data stored on your device. A Google Drive backup, if you made one, stays in your Drive until you delete it with “Delete all data” or remove this app’s hidden data in your Google Drive settings.

Sharing of Information

Your information is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone. The only place it can go is your own Google Drive, and only if you turn sync on.

Feedback and Links in the App

The Profile tab has round buttons for feedback, this Privacy Policy and the developer’s YouTube channel. They open in your email app, browser or another app on your phone. Feedback is sent only if you press send in your email app, and contains only what you write. Websites and apps you reach through these links have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Children’s Privacy

This application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the developer never receives any. Google Drive sync requires a Google account, which is subject to Google’s own age requirements. The application contains no advertising and no social features.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when the application gets new features. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be made live by updating the text directly on this hosted webpage, and the effective date above will change.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer at email@ashiqur.in. You can also visit the Activity Calendar page.
